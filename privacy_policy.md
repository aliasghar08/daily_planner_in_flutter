# Privacy Policy for MedDay Flow

**Effective Date:** September 29, 2026  
**Developer:** Ali Asghar  

Thank you for choosing **MedDay Flow** ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how your personal and health-related information is collected, used, and protected when you use our mobile application.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. Depending on how you use MedDay Flow, we may collect the following types of information:

* **Account Information:** When you register for an account, we collect your email address and authentication credentials securely through our backend service providers (such as Firebase).
* **User-Generated Content:** This includes the tasks, schedules, reminders, and medication tracking notes that you input into the app.
* **Health and Wellness Data:** Information related to your medication schedules and dosage times, which is stored locally or synchronized securely to assist you with your daily routines.
* **Device and Usage Data:** Basic diagnostic information, crash logs, and app performance metrics to help us improve stability and user experience.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

* To provide, maintain, and personalize the MedDay Flow application experience.
* To manage your account and deliver secure authentication (including biometric login features like Face ID or Touch ID).
* To send you scheduled local notifications and reminders for your tasks and medications.
* To troubleshoot bugs, monitor performance, and improve overall app functionality.

---

## 3. Data Storage and Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal and health data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Your data is transmitted securely using encrypted protocols (HTTPS/SSL) and stored securely via trusted cloud infrastructure.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services

MedDay Flow may integrate with external services to enhance your experience (such as Google Calendar, Google Tasks, or Apple Health). These services operate under their own privacy policies. We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

---

## 5. Your Choices and Data Rights

* **Access and Update:** You can view and update your account details and schedule items directly within the app settings.
* **Account Deletion:** You have the right to request the complete deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us directly.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

MedDay Flow is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can promptly delete it.

---

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
